About Us

We are a young technology company that believes the future lies in the integration of AI and robots into daily life. Our mission is to free people from routine by providing smart assistants and modern communication tools.

Currently, we are a fully digital company and do not have a physical office, but we plan to rent one in time to create a powerful tech hub for our team.

Founder's Manifesto

"Kvant" was born from the conviction that the technologies of the future should not wait for decades. We live in an era where age is just a number on a document, and real power lies in lines of code, bold engineering solutions, and the desire to be useful.

Our startup is a challenge to the status quo. We are building a digital ecosystem and robot prototypes today so that tomorrow they become a part of every home. We are not afraid to start small because we know: great IT empires were built in garages and behind the screens of ordinary enthusiasts' computers.

— Founder of Kvant Company
